Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy explains how ENDTECH AI SOLUTIONS handles personal information.

1. Overview

ENDTECH AI SOLUTIONS ("ENDTECH", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes what information we collect through this website and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit via forms — name, email, phone, company and message content.
  • Technical data such as browser type, device and approximate region, used for performance and security.
  • Preferences such as your theme choice, stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and never transmitted to us.

3. How We Use Information

  • Responding to inquiries and preparing project proposals.
  • Delivering and supporting contracted services.
  • Improving the security, performance and content of this website.
  • Meeting legal or regulatory obligations where required.

4. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.
  • We do not collect payment card details on this website.
  • We do not store passwords or sensitive credentials in the browser.

5. Data Storage & Security

Information submitted through our contact form is transmitted to and processed by our secure backend systems, protected with access controls and encryption in transit. We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law.

6. Third-Party Services

This website loads fonts and icon libraries from public content delivery networks (Google Fonts, jsDelivr). These providers may receive standard technical request data (such as your IP address) when the page loads.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details on our contact page. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our contact page.
